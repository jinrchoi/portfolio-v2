Jin translates novels and narrative essays from Korean to English, mainly drawn to works that contain queer and feminist themes, social critique, transnational histories, and introspective reflection.

Represented by Ivan Mulcahy and Seo Park at International Creative Agency.

The samples and synopses below are available for inquiry by editors. Please contact Jin for details.

Novels

Samples and synopses · Fiction

  • Women on Earth (지상의 여자들) by Park Moon-young — feminist speculative fiction
  • And So To Pohang We Go (이렇게 된 이상 포항으로 간다) by Bora Chung and Choi Uitaek — mystery and thriller

  • Last Words from Barcelona (바르셀로나의 유서) by Baek Sehee — autobiographical novella on struggling with self-love
  • Heading to Kimbap Heaven (김밥천국 가는 날) by Jeon Heyjin — healing fiction and social critique on gender and labor
  • Korea’s Monster Management Agency (사단법인 한국괴물관리협회) by Bae Yeram — contemporary twist on Korean folklore and mythology
